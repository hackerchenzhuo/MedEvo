01 — THE PROBLEM & THE IDEA

A wrong answer does not make every step wrong

Medical reasoning connects image findings, clinical context, and knowledge. A student can make several valid observations before one local error sends the answer off course. Discarding the whole trajectory loses those useful steps.

The idea

Keep the supported prefix and repair from the first consequential error. Medical tags identify the clinical context; an error diagnosis identifies what to revisit. A minimal, answer-free hint guides the same student to generate its own correction.

02 — THE KEY INSIGHT

The easiest repair is not always the best lesson

The longest failed trajectories are harder to repair than randomly selected failures: 55.70% versus 67.50% repair success. Yet they yield higher downstream accuracy: 66.78% versus 64.35%.

Useful supervision depends on what the student learns from a correction. Independent feedback reveals which weaknesses remain, so the next round can focus on failures that matter beyond the training examples.

03 — THE SELF-IMPROVEMENT LOOP

Local recovery, guided by medical knowledge

1 · Locate

Step uncertainty proposes where to inspect. An auditor checks the evidence, diagnoses the first consequential error, and identifies the valid prefix.

2 · Guide

The auditor uses observable medical tags and the local error to give a minimal recovery hint, without accessing the reference answer.

3 · Continue

The same student keeps the prefix and regenerates the suffix. Correctness, evidence support, and learnability determine which repairs become supervision.

4 · Evolve

Independent feedback across medical tags and error types guides the next data selection. The updated supervision carries experience across rounds.

06 — ANALYSIS

What makes the improvement continue?

Preserve useful reasoning

Type-guided suffix continuation reaches 71.80% repair success and 67.22% downstream accuracy, outperforming full rewriting.

Keep feedback independent

Training-error feedback ends at 64.01% benchmark accuracy. Independent feedback reaches 69.12%, directing learning toward transferable weaknesses.

Use moderate guidance

Three hints perform best in the hint-count comparison. Adding more guidance reduces downstream accuracy, reinforcing the value of targeted recovery.

Reproduce the gains

All three independent runs improve through R3, reaching 69.09% ± 0.24 mean accuracy across the seven benchmarks.
